# APZ LEGAL — EMAIL MANAGEMENT, INGESTION & MATTER LINKING IMPLEMENTATION

## OBJECTIVE

Implement the APZ Legal email functionality in accordance with **APZ Legal PRD v2 (Revised + Aligned)**.

Email must NOT be treated as a standalone generic inbox.

The primary purpose of email functionality is to make email correspondence a **secure, matter-linked legal record** that can be searched, viewed, audited and used by the APZ Legal AI within the appropriate matter context.

The target architecture is:

```text
External Email Provider
        ↓
Email Ingestion
        ↓
Email Processing
        ↓
Matter / Client Matching
        ↓
Matter Workspace
        ↓
Emails
        ↓
APZ Legal AI / Search / Audit
```

Outbound communication should separately follow:

```text
AI / User
   ↓
Email Draft
   ↓
Human Review
   ↓
Send
   ↓
Audit Log
```

# 2. PRD ALIGNMENT

The PRD currently defines:

### Matter Workspace

The Matter Workspace includes:

* Documents
* Tasks
* Emails
* Research
* Billing
* AI conversations
* Audit trail

Therefore, every relevant matter should have an **Emails** area.

The PRD also identifies:

### Phase 1D — Integrations

* Email ingestion
* Legal research engine
* Citation validation

The email implementation must therefore support the ingestion and matter-linking foundation required for Phase 1D.

---

# 3. EMAIL SHOULD BE MATTER-CENTRIC

Do NOT design this as:

```text
APZ Legal → Generic Email Inbox
```

Design it as:

```text
APZ Legal
   ↓
Matter
   ↓
Emails
```

The email record should be linked to:

* Matter where applicable
* Client where applicable
* Users/participants where applicable
* Documents/attachments where applicable

The system should preserve the original email information.

* Provider

# 5. EMAIL INGESTION

Implement an email ingestion service.

The service should be designed so that an external provider can be connected without changing the rest of APZ Legal.

Do not hard-code the application to one provider if this can reasonably be avoided.

Create an abstraction similar to:

```text
EmailProvider
    ↓
EmailIngestionService
    ↓
EmailProcessor
    ↓
MatterLinkingService
    ↓
APZ Legal Email Repository
```

The initial provider should be selected based on the existing APZ Legal environment and requirements.

If no provider is currently configured, implement the provider abstraction and configuration structure without pretending that live ingestion works.

Do not use mock emails as production functionality.

---

# 6. MATTER MATCHING

This is a core APZ Legal requirement.

When an email is ingested, determine whether it can be associated with an existing matter.

Matching should use reliable information such as:

### Primary matching

* Matter number/reference in subject/body
* Existing email thread associated with a matter

### Secondary matching

* Sender
* Recipient
* Client
* Opposing party
* Known matter participants
* Subject
* Existing correspondence history

### AI-assisted matching

AI may suggest a matter when deterministic matching is insufficient.

Example:

```text
Potential Matter Match

Matter:
Smith v Jones
MTR-2026-0148

Confidence:
92%

Reason:
Sender and recipient match known matter participants and
the email subject is consistent with existing correspondence.

[Link to Matter]
[Choose Different Matter]
[Leave Unlinked]
```

Do not allow AI to silently create high-confidence legal associations without appropriate safeguards.

Make the matching behaviour configurable.

---

# 7. EMAIL LINKING

Users must be able to manually link an email to a matter.

Provide:

* Link to Matter
* Change Matter
* Remove Matter Link
* Leave Unlinked

When manually linked, record the action in the audit trail.

The system should also support configurable automatic linking where the confidence/rules are sufficient.

* Recipients
* CC
* Date/time

* Body

---

# 10. THREADING

Support email conversations/threads.

Where the provider exposes thread/conversation IDs, preserve them.

Allow the user to view related messages as a conversation.

Do not merge unrelated emails merely because they have similar subjects.

Use provider thread identifiers plus appropriate message metadata.

---

# 11. ATTACHMENTS

Email attachments must be handled securely.

For each attachment:

* Preserve filename
* Preserve MIME type
* Preserve source email
* Preserve external attachment ID where available
* Store securely
* Associate with the email
* Allow authorised users to access it

Where an attachment is relevant to the matter, it should be accessible from the matter's Documents area.

However:

DO NOT silently create duplicate document records.

If the existing document system supports document ingestion, integrate with that system.

Maintain the relationship:

```text
Matter
   ↓
Email
   ↓
Attachment
   ↓
Document
```

The original email must remain traceable as the source.

# 12. EMAIL SEARCH

Implement secure email search.

Users should be able to search by:

* Subject
* Sender
* Recipient
* Matter
* Client
* Date
* Thread
* Attachment name
* Email body where supported

Search results must respect:

* RBAC

* User permissions

# 13. AI INTEGRATION

This is a major requirement.

The existing APZ Legal AI should eventually be able to reason over emails within the user's authorized matter context.

Examples:

> "Summarise the latest correspondence."

> "Find all correspondence with the opposing attorney."

> "What did the client say about the settlement?"

> "When did the client first raise this issue?"

> "Summarise the email correspondence from the last two weeks."

The AI must retrieve the underlying emails using the existing authorized data-access architecture.

Do not give the AI unrestricted database access.

Use controlled backend tools/services.

Architecture:

```text
APZ Legal AI
     ↓
Email Search Tool
     ↓
Authorization
     ↓
Email Service
     ↓
Authorized Emails
     ↓
AI Context
```

---

# 14. AI CITATIONS

Where the AI uses email information to answer a question, provide a reference to the underlying email where the existing AI citation architecture supports this.

The user should be able to identify:

* Email subject
* Date
* Sender
* Matter
* Relevant source

This aligns with the PRD requirement for citation enforcement and AI decision traceability.

Do not invent citations.

---

# 15. EMAIL DRAFTING

The PRD explicitly classifies:

**Email drafting = LOW RISK AI**

The existing APZ Legal AI should be able to generate email drafts.

Example:

User:

> "Draft an email to the client explaining the next steps."

The AI should generate a draft.

The draft must clearly be a draft.

The system should NOT send the email automatically.

The lawyer must be able to:

* Review
* Edit
* Save draft
* Discard
* Send

If outbound email functionality already exists, integrate with it.

If it does not exist, create the architecture for it without pretending it is production-ready until an actual provider is configured.

Outbound email flow:

```text
AI/User
   ↓
Draft
   ↓
Review
   ↓
User confirmation
   ↓
Send
   ↓
Provider
   ↓
Sent Email
   ↓
Matter
   ↓

```

Never automatically send an AI-generated email merely because the AI generated it.

---

# 17. AI + EMAIL CONFIRMATION

The following distinction must be enforced:

### Draft request

User:

> "Draft an email to the client."

Result:

**Draft created.**

No confirmation needed to create the draft.

### Send request

User:

> "Send the email to the client."

Result:

The system should display the email and request explicit confirmation if required by the application's approval policy.

Example:

> "The email is ready to send to John Smith. Would you like me to send it?"

Only explicit confirmation should trigger sending.

# 18. ROLE-BASED ACCESS

Use the existing APZ Legal RBAC.

At minimum, consider the PRD's role responsibilities:

### Secretary

* Email/matter linking support
* Access to permitted matter correspondence
* Filing correspondence

### Paralegal

* Document/email handling
* Matter correspondence support

### Candidate Attorney

* Restricted access based on assigned matter permissions

### Associate Attorney

* Draft correspondence
* Manage correspondence for permitted matters

### Partner

* Matter correspondence
* Appropriate review/approval

### Managing Partner

* Firm-wide access according to policy

### Billing Officer

Do not automatically provide unrestricted access to confidential email content.

### Compliance Officer

Access according to compliance/audit requirements.

The backend must enforce authorization.

Do not rely on frontend hiding.

---

# 19. MATTER CONFIDENTIALITY

Email access must follow the same confidentiality model as matter access.

If User A cannot access Matter X:

User A must NOT be able to:

* View Matter X emails
* Search Matter X emails
* Ask AI to retrieve Matter X emails
* Download Matter X attachments
* Retrieve Matter X email metadata

This must be enforced server-side.

---

# 20. AUDIT LOGGING

Email activity must integrate with the existing APZ Legal audit system.

Audit important actions including:

* Email ingested
* Email linked to matter
* Email unlinked
* Matter association changed
* Attachment accessed where required by policy
* Email draft generated by AI
* Email edited
* Email sent
* Email deleted/archived where supported
* AI searched email
* Unauthorized email access attempt
* Administrative email configuration changes

Capture where appropriate:

* User
* Timestamp
* Action
* Matter ID
* Email ID
* Source/provider
* AI involvement
* Authorization result
* Correlation/request ID

Do not log sensitive email body content unnecessarily.

---

# 21. AI GOVERNANCE

Email drafting is Low Risk according to the PRD.

However:

* AI-generated content must be identifiable as AI-generated.
* Human review must remain possible.
* AI explanation/citation mechanisms should be preserved.
* AI actions must be traceable.
* Existing confidence/risk controls must remain intact.

Do not classify all email-related AI operations as the same risk.

For example:

**Summarising an email → Low Risk**

**Drafting an email → Low Risk**

**Making a consequential legal recommendation based on emails → appropriate higher-risk classification according to the existing AI governance framework.**

---

# 22. EMAIL + DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT

When an email contains an attachment:

```text
Email
  ↓
Attachment
  ↓
Document System
  ↓
Matter
```

The attachment should remain traceable to its originating email.

If the document is subsequently:

* reviewed
* AI analysed
* approved
* signed
* archived

those workflows must continue using the existing Document Management system.

Do not create a second document lifecycle inside Email.

---

# 23. EMAIL + KNOWLEDGE BASE

Do NOT automatically place all client/matter emails into the firm's general knowledge base.

Matter correspondence and firm knowledge are different data domains.

Email content should remain within the appropriate:

**Client → Matter → Email**

context unless a specific approved process exists to promote information into the firm knowledge base.

Any future knowledge-base ingestion should require explicit rules/approval.

---

# 24. EMAIL + POPIA / CONFIDENTIALITY

Treat email as confidential legal information.

Ensure:

* Access controls
* Encryption through existing infrastructure
* Secure storage
* Secure transmission
* Appropriate retention
* Auditability
* No unauthorized AI exposure
* No cross-matter leakage

Do not expose email content to external AI services without following the application's existing AI/data-processing controls.

---

# 25. EMAIL CONFIGURATION

Create an appropriate administrative configuration area if one does not already exist.

Administrators should eventually be able to configure:

* Email provider
* Connected mailbox
* Ingestion status
* Sync status
* Last successful sync
* Error status
* Connection status
* Sync frequency/webhook status where applicable

Do not expose provider secrets to ordinary users.

---

# 26. BACKGROUND PROCESSING

If the existing application supports background workers/queues, use them for email ingestion.

Do not make a user wait for an entire mailbox synchronization request.

Conceptually:

```text
Provider
   ↓
Webhook/Polling
   ↓
Queue
   ↓
Email Processor
   ↓
Database
```

The exact implementation should follow the existing APZ Legal architecture.

---

# 27. IDEMPOTENCY

Email ingestion must be idempotent.

If the same email is received multiple times, APZ Legal must not create duplicate records.

Use stable provider identifiers.

Test duplicate ingestion explicitly.

---

# 28. SECURITY TESTING

Test:

### User access

User without matter access attempts to retrieve email.

Expected:

**403/unauthorized response.**

### AI access

User asks:

> "Show me emails from a matter I cannot access."

Expected:

AI cannot retrieve them.

### Attachment access

Unauthorized user attempts to access attachment.

Expected:

Denied.

### Search

Unauthorized user searches for confidential email.

Expected:

Email does not appear.

### Sending

User attempts to send without required authorization/confirmation.

Expected:

Blocked.

# 33. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

The implementation will be considered successful when:

### Matter

A user can open a matter and access:

**Overview | Documents | Emails | Tasks | Research | Billing | AI | Audit**

where the Emails section is available according to permissions.

### Email

An authorized user can:

* View emails
* Search emails
* View threads
* View attachments
* Link emails to matters
* Change email matter associations where authorized

### AI

The authorized AI assistant can eventually:

* Search permitted emails
* Summarise permitted emails
* Answer questions using permitted email correspondence
* Produce appropriate source references
* Draft emails

### Security

Unauthorized users cannot access restricted matter emails.

### Audit

Important email actions are recorded in the APZ Legal audit system.

### Outbound

AI-generated emails are never silently sent.

### Regression

Existing APZ Legal functionality continues to work.

APZ Legal Email is NOT simply an email inbox.

It is:

**EMAIL → MATTER → CLIENT → DOCUMENTS → AI → WORKFLOW → AUDIT**

and every part of that chain must respect:

**Authentication + RBAC + Matter Permissions + Legal Confidentiality + AI Governance + Auditability.**
