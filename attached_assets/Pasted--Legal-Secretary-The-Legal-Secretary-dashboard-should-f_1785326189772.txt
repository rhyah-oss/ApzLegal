### Legal Secretary:

The Legal Secretary dashboard should focus on daily coordination, scheduling, appointments, visitors, and administrative Matter support.

Show:

* Today's Appointments
* Upcoming Appointments
* Visitors Expected Today
* Appointments Requiring Confirmation
* Unlinked Emails
* Emails Awaiting Matter Association
* Tasks Requiring Coordination
* Documents Requiring Administrative Action

## APPOINTMENTS

Allow Legal Secretaries to:

* Create appointments
* Schedule appointments for attorneys/partners
* Assign an appointment to a specific attorney or staff member
* Link an appointment to a Client
* Link an appointment to a Matter
* Add external attendees
* Add appointment location
* Add virtual meeting details
* Add notes
* Set date and time
* Set appointment duration
* Set appointment status

Appointment statuses:

* Scheduled
* Confirmed
* Rescheduled
* Cancelled
* Completed
* No Show

Examples:

Client Consultation
→ Assigned to: Partner Name
→ Client: ABC Holdings
→ Matter: MAT-0012
→ Date: 28 July 2026
→ Time: 10:00–11:00
→ Location: Boardroom 1
→ Status: Confirmed

## VISITOR MANAGEMENT

Allow the Legal Secretary to register expected visitors.

Capture:

* Visitor name
* Company / organisation
* Contact information
* Person they are visiting
* Assigned attorney or staff member
* Client relationship where applicable
* Matter where applicable
* Expected arrival time
* Expected departure time
* Purpose of visit
* Visitor status

Visitor statuses:

* Expected
* Checked In
* With Attorney
* Checked Out
* Cancelled
* No Show

Example:

Visitor:
John Smith
Company:
ABC Holdings
Visiting:
Partner Name
Matter:
MAT-0012
Expected:
10:00
Status:
Expected

When the visitor arrives, the Secretary should be able to mark:

CHECKED IN

When the visit ends:

CHECKED OUT

## APPOINTMENT AND VISITOR ASSIGNMENT

Appointments and visitors should be assignable to:

* Partner
* Managing Partner
* Associate Attorney
* Candidate Attorney
* Other authorised staff

The assigned person should see the appointment or visitor in their own dashboard/calendar.

The Legal Secretary should have an overview of all appointments and expected visitors for the firm, subject to their permissions.

## MATTER INTEGRATION

Where relevant, appointments and visitors should be linked to:

* Client
* Matter
* Assigned Staff Member

The activity should then be visible in the relevant Matter timeline.

Example:

Client Meeting Scheduled
→ Client: ABC Holdings
→ Matter: MAT-0012
→ Assigned Attorney: Partner Name
→ Date/Time
→ Location

## IMPORTANT

This should extend the existing Calendar and Task functionality rather than creating a completely separate scheduling system.

Appointments should integrate with the existing Calendar.

Visitors should integrate with the appointment where applicable.

Appointments and visitors should be auditable where they relate to a Client, Matter, or legal activity.

Do not create a public-facing visitor management system. This is an internal law-firm administrative workflow for Legal Secretaries and authorised staff.
