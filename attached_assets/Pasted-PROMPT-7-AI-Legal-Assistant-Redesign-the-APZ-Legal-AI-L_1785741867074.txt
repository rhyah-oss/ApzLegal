PROMPT 7 — AI Legal Assistant
Redesign the APZ Legal AI Legal Assistant to align with the AI Governance Framework in the PRD v2 and User Journey 2.
IMPORTANT:
The AI Assistant must NOT function as a generic chatbot.
It must be a governed legal AI system operating within the context of a Matter.
AI RISK CLASSIFICATION
LOW RISK:
Email drafting
Summaries
MEDIUM RISK:
Contracts
Clause recommendations
HIGH RISK:
Legal opinions
Litigation guidance
Display the risk classification prominently for every applicable AI workflow.
AI OUTPUT METADATA
Every legal AI output must show:
Risk Level
Confidence Score
Citations
Citation Verification Status
AI-generated content status
Human Review Status
Approval Status where applicable
Example:
Risk: Medium
Confidence: 87%
Citations: 4
Citation Verification: Required
AI Content: Detected
Human Review: Pending
Do not fabricate confidence scores or citations.
If these values are not genuinely generated by the backend, clearly mark them as unavailable rather than displaying fake values.
AI WORKFLOWS
Create purpose-specific AI workflows:
Draft Contract
Summarise Matter
Draft Email
Analyse Clause
Legal Research
Generate Matter Summary
Do not make every action a generic chat.
CONTRACT DRAFTING WORKFLOW
Implement:
Select Matter
→ Select Contract Type
→ Select Jurisdiction
→ Select Relevant Clauses
→ Generate Draft
→ Show Risk
→ Show Confidence
→ Show Citations
→ Citation Verification
→ Attorney Review
→ Edit
→ Save to Matter
→ Initiate Approval
GOVERNANCE
Implement tracking for:
AI explanation metadata
Citations
Confidence
Human reviewer
Human override
Approval
AI decision traceability
High-risk AI outputs must require appropriate human review before being treated as approved legal work.
MATTER INTEGRATION
All AI conversations and generated content must be linked to the Matter.
AI activity must appear in the Matter timeline and Audit Log.
Do not allow AI-generated legal content to bypass document governance.
