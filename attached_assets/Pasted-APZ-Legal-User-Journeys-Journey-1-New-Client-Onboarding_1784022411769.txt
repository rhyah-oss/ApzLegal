APZ Legal - User Journeys
Journey 1: New Client Onboarding &amp; Matter Creation
Actor
Attorney / Legal Secretary
Objective
Create a new client profile, complete compliance checks, and open a legal matter.
Flow
1. User logs into APZ Legal.
2. User navigates to Clients.
3. User selects &quot;Create New Client&quot;.
4. User captures:
o Personal details
o Contact information
o Identification information
o Company information (if applicable)
5. User uploads FICA documents.
6. System validates required documentation.
7. User submits client profile.
8. System performs conflict check.
9. Potential conflicts are flagged.
10. Partner reviews conflict results.
11. Partner approves matter creation.
12. Matter is created.
13. Matter workspace is generated.
14. Attorney begins working on the matter.
Outcome
Client successfully onboarded and matter opened.

Journey 2: AI Assisted Contract Drafting

Actor
Attorney
Objective
Generate a first draft of a legal contract.
Flow
1. Attorney opens a matter.
2. Attorney selects AI Legal Assistant.
3. Attorney chooses:
o Contract type
o Jurisdiction
o Relevant clauses
4. AI generates draft.
5. System displays:
o Draft document
o Confidence score
o Citation references
6. Attorney reviews draft.
7. Attorney edits content.
8. Document saved to matter.
9. Approval workflow initiated.
Outcome
Draft contract prepared and submitted for review.
Added
● AI risk classification display
● Citation verification step before review submission
● Mandatory AI content tagging

Journey 3: Document Approval Workflow
Actor

Partner
Objective
Approve legal documentation before client release.
Flow
1. Attorney submits document for approval.
2. Partner receives notification.
3. Partner opens document.
4. Partner reviews:
o Content
o AI-generated sections
o Citations
5. Partner chooses:
o Approve
o Reject
o Request Changes
6. System records decision.
7. Audit trail updated.
Outcome
Document approved or returned for revision.

Journey 4: Legal Research Using AI
Actor
Attorney
Objective
Research a legal issue using firm knowledge and external legal sources.
Flow
1. Attorney opens Research Module.
2. Attorney enters legal query.
3. System searches:

o Internal precedents
o Knowledge Base
o Case law
o Legislation
4. AI summarises findings.
5. AI provides:
o Case references
o Relevant legislation
o Suggested precedents
6. Attorney saves findings to matter.
Outcome
Research completed and attached to matter.
Enhanced :
● AI confidence scoring added to results
● Research traceability saved to matter timeline

Journey 5: Email Discovery &amp; Matter Linking
Actor
Legal Secretary
Objective
Associate incoming emails with the correct matter.
Flow
1. Email received via Outlook or Microsoft 365.
2. APZ Legal imports email.
3. System suggests related matter.
4. User confirms association.
5. Attachments extracted automatically.
6. Email added to matter timeline.
7. AI generates draft response.

8. User reviews and sends response.
Outcome
Matter communication history maintained.

Journey 6: Time Tracking &amp; Billing
Actor
Attorney
Objective
Capture billable time for legal work.
Flow
1. Attorney opens matter.
2. Attorney starts timer.
3. Attorney performs legal work.
4. Timer stopped upon completion.
5. Activity description added.
6. Time entry linked to matter.
7. Billing team reviews entries.
8. Invoice generated.
Outcome
Billable time captured accurately.

Journey 7: Digital Signature Workflow
Actor
Attorney &amp; Client
Objective
Obtain legally binding signatures.
Flow
1. Attorney uploads final document.
2. Attorney selects &quot;Send for Signature&quot;.

3. APZ Sign workflow created.
4. Client receives secure signing link.
5. Client reviews document.
6. Client signs electronically.
7. Signature certificate generated.
8. Signed document stored in matter.
Outcome
Document legally executed.
