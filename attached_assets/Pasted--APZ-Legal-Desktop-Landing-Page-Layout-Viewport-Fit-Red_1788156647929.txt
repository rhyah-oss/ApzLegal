### APZ Legal — Desktop Landing Page Layout & Viewport Fit

Redesign the **APZ Legal landing page** so that the entire primary landing-page experience is **perfectly centered, polished, balanced, and fully visible within a standard desktop viewport without vertical scrolling**.

#### Core Requirement

The landing page must fit within **one desktop viewport**.

* No vertical page scrolling on desktop.
* No content should be cut off at the bottom.
* The user should be able to see the complete landing-page composition immediately after loading.
* Design primarily for **1440 × 900**, while remaining responsive for 1280 × 720 and larger desktop screens.
* Do NOT simply shrink everything to make it fit.
* Instead, intelligently restructure spacing, sizing, and hierarchy so the page naturally fits.

### Overall Visual Direction

APZ Legal should feel:

* Premium
* Corporate
* Sophisticated
* Modern legal technology
* Minimal
* Trustworthy
* High-end SaaS
* Similar in quality to leading enterprise legal-tech platforms

Avoid anything that looks:

* Playful
* Startup-template-like
* Overly rounded
* Cartoonish
* Excessively colourful
* Cluttered
* Like a generic AI landing page

Use a restrained **black / charcoal / warm beige / white / subtle gold accent** visual language consistent with APZ Legal's professional legal identity.

### Desktop Layout

Create a structured full-screen composition:

#### 1. Header

Keep the header compact and horizontally aligned.

Left:

* APZ Legal logo/wordmark

Center/right navigation:

* Platform
* Solutions
* AI
* Security
* About

Right:

* Sign In
* Request Demo / Get Started

Header should consume approximately **70–80px maximum** of vertical space.

Do not create a large oversized navigation bar.

---

#### 2. Hero Section

The hero should be the dominant visual area.

Use a **two-column desktop composition**:

LEFT:

* Small professional eyebrow label such as:
  "LEGAL OPERATIONS • AI • COMPLIANCE"
* Strong headline
* Short supporting description
* Primary CTA
* Secondary CTA

RIGHT:

* A sophisticated APZ Legal product preview/dashboard mockup.

The hero should be vertically centered within the remaining viewport.

Example hierarchy:

**Legal work, intelligently connected.**

A unified legal operating workspace for matters, documents, compliance, research, AI assistance, workflows, billing and more.

[Request a Demo] [Explore Platform]

Do not make the headline excessively large. It should feel premium rather than promotional.

---

#### 3. Product Preview

The product preview should look like an actual APZ Legal interface rather than a generic dashboard illustration.

Show a refined legal workspace containing elements such as:

* Matter overview
* Client information
* Matter status
* Recent documents
* AI assistant/research panel
* Tasks
* Compliance indicators

Keep this preview visually contained.

It must NOT create additional page height.

Use subtle depth, borders and shadows rather than huge decorative graphics.

---

#### 4. Bottom Trust / Capability Strip

At the bottom of the viewport, add a compact capability strip rather than another large section.

For example:

**MATTERS** · **LEGAL AI** · **DOCUMENTS** · **COMPLIANCE** · **WORKFLOWS** · **TIME & BILLING**

This should reinforce the breadth of APZ Legal without requiring the user to scroll.

Keep it compact and elegant.

---

### Viewport Rules

Treat the landing page as a **100vh desktop experience**.

Use:

```css
min-height: 100vh;
height: 100vh;
overflow: hidden;
```

for the desktop landing-page shell where appropriate.

However, do not blindly force `overflow: hidden` if it causes content to become inaccessible.

The correct solution is to ensure the content actually fits.

Use responsive calculations such as:

```css
min-height: calc(100vh - header-height);
```

and carefully control:

* Hero padding
* Typography
* Image/mockup dimensions
* Navigation height
* Vertical gaps
* CTA spacing
* Bottom strip height

### Important Responsive Behaviour

For desktop:

**1280 × 720**

* Everything must still fit.
* Reduce spacing and preview dimensions slightly.
* No vertical scrolling.

**1440 × 900**

* This should be the ideal presentation.
* Plenty of breathing room.
* Everything vertically centered.

**1920 × 1080**

* Do NOT allow the content to become excessively stretched.
* Keep the hero at a controlled maximum width.
* Maintain a premium composition rather than spreading elements across the entire screen.

For tablet/mobile:

* It is acceptable for the page to become vertically scrollable.
* Do NOT compromise the desktop composition to accommodate mobile.

### Container

Use a controlled maximum-width container:

```css
max-width: 1400px;
margin: 0 auto;
padding-inline: 40px;
```

Adjust the values based on the existing design system.

The landing page should feel **centered within the screen**, not pinned to the left.

### Spacing

Use intentional spacing.

Do not use large empty vertical gaps.

The visual rhythm should be approximately:

Header
↓
Hero content
↓
Product preview
↓
Capability strip

Everything should feel balanced inside the viewport.

### Typography

Use a professional modern sans-serif.

Create a strong hierarchy:

* Eyebrow: small and tracked
* H1: large but controlled
* Supporting text: readable and restrained
* Buttons: compact
* Navigation: understated

Avoid enormous H1 typography that forces the rest of the page below the fold.

### Eliminate Unnecessary Sections

For the desktop landing page, do NOT stack multiple marketing sections such as:

* Huge feature sections
* Long AI explanations
* Testimonials
* Large pricing sections
* Repeated CTA sections
* Large footer content

Those can exist on other pages or below the fold if required, but the **primary landing viewport must remain clean and immediately understandable**.

### Visual Quality Requirements

The final result must look intentionally designed at a desktop resolution.

Check the page at:

* 1280 × 720
* 1366 × 768
* 1440 × 900
* 1920 × 1080

At each resolution:

1. No unwanted horizontal scrolling.
2. No unwanted vertical scrolling.
3. No clipped hero content.
4. No overlapping elements.
5. No excessive whitespace.
6. Hero remains visually centered.
7. Header remains compact.
8. CTA buttons remain visible.
9. Product preview remains fully visible.
10. Bottom capability strip remains visible.

### Do Not

* Do not just reduce the entire page using CSS transforms.
* Do not use `scale()` to force the UI to fit.
* Do not make the typography microscopic.
* Do not create a giant hero image that pushes content below the fold.
* Do not add unnecessary decorative elements.
* Do not introduce excessive rounded cards.
* Do not make the page look like a template.
* Do not create a page where the user has to scroll to understand the product.

### Final Objective

When someone opens APZ Legal on a desktop monitor, the **entire first impression should be visible immediately**.

It should look like a polished enterprise legal platform:

**Logo → Navigation → Strong Hero → Product Interface Preview → Core Capabilities**

All contained within one clean, balanced desktop viewport.

The result should feel **finished, intentional, premium and production-ready**, not like a collection of sections placed one underneath another.
