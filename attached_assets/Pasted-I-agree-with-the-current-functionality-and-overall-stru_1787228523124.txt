I agree with the current functionality and overall structure, but the visual language still feels too colourful, busy and slightly juvenile for a professional legal application.

I want to refine the existing design toward a **sleek, restrained, corporate legal-tech aesthetic**.

The core issue is not the light/dark themes themselves. It is the combination of **too many colours, too many containers, excessive visual treatments and inconsistent spacing**.

Please apply these principles across the application:

* Reduce the number of colours used in the UI.
* Use the existing theme/brand colours primarily for navigation, primary actions and important highlights.
* Reserve red, yellow, green and other semantic colours strictly for meaningful status information.
* Tone down semantic colours so they feel sophisticated rather than bright and playful.
* Reduce unnecessary cards and nested containers.
* Reduce excessive borders and shadows.
* Reduce excessive rounded corners.
* Increase intentional whitespace between major sections.
* Strengthen typography and hierarchy instead of using colour to create emphasis.
* Use tables, lists and clean information rows where they communicate information better than multiple cards.
* Make dashboard metrics more restrained and editorial.
* Make badges and status indicators smaller and less visually dominant.
* Ensure primary actions are obvious without making the entire interface colourful.
* Keep layouts clean, aligned and consistent across modules.

The desired personality is:

**Professional · Corporate · Legal · Intelligent · Minimal · Premium · Restrained**

It should feel appropriate for a serious law firm using the application every day.

It should NOT feel:

**Playful · Colourful · Bubbly · Consumer-oriented · Over-designed**

Do not change the application's functionality, navigation, Dev Mode or overall product structure.

Do not redesign the light/dark themes themselves.

This is a **visual refinement of the existing application**, with particular emphasis on colour restraint, spacing, hierarchy and reducing visual clutter.

Please establish consistent design tokens/components for these principles and then apply them throughout the application rather than making isolated page-by-page changes.
